Language Focus
Grammar Notes — Nhưng / Mà (lại) & Lại 📐 Grammar
Presenting two contrasting propositions: [S1 + V1] nhưng / mà + (S2) + lại + V2

Foregrounds that proposition 2 is unexpected or the opposite of proposition 1.

Tôi đặt cỡ M mà lại nhận được cỡ S. — “I ordered size M but I received size S.” Receiving size S was disappointing and unexpected.
Tôi đặt cỡ M nhưng shop lại gửi cỡ S. — “I ordered size M but the shop sent me size S.” The speaker emphasizes that the shop's action was disappointing and wrong.
V + lại — “again” / “back”

To carry on an action one more time (“again”):

To do something in return (“back”):

Chị nói lại tên người nhận giúp em được không ạ? (Can you say the recipient's name again?)
Chị gửi lại hàng rồi em sẽ ship đồ mới cho chị nhé! (Send the package back and I'll ship the new item to you!)
Lại + V · (Vì sao) lại V?

To do something again, often with frustration:

To describe an action opposite to what one expects:

Đơn hôm 6/3 lại muộn nữa và cái quần tôi đặt bị lỗi.
Tôi đặt cỡ M mà lại nhận được cỡ S. · Vì sao lại thế? (Why is that so?)

Activity 12 Pronunciation: ac – ach – ec 🗣 Tongue Twister
Thử đọc các câu sau rồi nghe bản ghi âm để so sánh và xem từ nào mình đọc chưa được rõ. Tập đọc cho trôi chảy rồi ghi âm.
Try reading the sentences, then listen to the recording to compare and see which words weren't clear. Practice reading fluently, then record yourself.
Pronunciation notes: ac vs ach vs ec
Even though written with “a”, the vowel in “ach” is a short /a/ (as in “ă”), so the vowel in ach is shorter than in ac. Vowel /e/ (“e”) is narrower and higher than /a/ (“a”).
Minimal pairs: các (many) · cách (ways) · khác (different) · khách (guest) · eng éc (squealing — onomatopoeia, a pig's scream) · anh ách (upset — lit. bloated, a frustrated feeling)
🔊 Tongue twister (SA)
  1. Heo kêu eng éc, anh Khang anh Hạnh tức anh ách.
  2. Các cách khác chẳng bằng các cách cũ.
  3. Các khách khác muốn các cách khác.

Culture & Varieties
🏮 Cultural Notes — Inbox & “Tiền Mất Tật Mang”
“Inbox | ib | nhắn tin”
Many online shops in Vietnam use Facebook and TikTok as their main platforms. Sellers livestream or post images on social media, and buyers “inbox” — send the shop a private message — to ask about products and prices or to make purchases.
“Tiền mất tật mang”
Literally “lose money, get an injury”, it can be translated as “money lost, efforts in vain”. It expresses regret or frustration when someone invests in something and not only fails to get good results but also ends up annoyed or worse off.
🗣 Language Varieties — Shopping Loan Words

Many shopping terms are loan words from English. Both the Vietnamese and English terms are commonly used; even when the English spelling is kept in writing, the pronunciation is localized.
