Write a Restaurant Review
to Post on Social Media

Viết đánh giá nhà hàng để đăng lên mạng xã hội

About This Task

You recently visited a restaurant and had a notable dining experience. Write a detailed review covering the food, ambiance, service, and overall experience, to be shared on social media so others can decide whether to visit. Include specific details about what you liked or disliked, and give a recommendation for potential diners.

Pre-Task
Read four real restaurant reviews & collect positive and negative adjectives
Main Task
Brainstorm, draft a 100–150-word review, then give peer feedback
Post-Task
Revise and post the review, then turn it into a 3–5 minute video

Write a Restaurant Review to Post on Social Media ✍️ Main Task

You recently visited a restaurant and had a notable dining experience. Your task is to write a detailed review of the restaurant, covering the food, ambiance, service, and overall experience. Grammar Notes — Reviewing & Evaluating 📐 Grammar
Không những / không chỉ … mà còn — “not only … but also”
Đồ ăn ở đây không những ngon mà còn rẻ.
Khiến cho / làm + Object + Adj — “make somebody + Adj”
Thái độ của nhân viên phục vụ khiến cho tôi thất vọng.
Chất lượng đồ ăn ở đây làm mình rất hài lòng.
Connecting words: nói chung (in general), dù thế nào (regardless)
Nói chung, đồ ăn ở đây tạm được.
Dù thế nào thì mình cũng sẽ quay lại đây với bạn bè.
Subject + không / chẳng + Adj + mấy — “not very …”
Thái độ của nhân viên phục vụ chẳng nhiệt tình mấy.
Đồ ăn ở quán này không rẻ mấy.

Culture & Varieties
🏮 Cultural Notes — English Words in Young People's Vietnamese
“View”, “DJ”, “chill”
In contemporary Vietnam, there is a growing trend among young people to incorporate English words into their vocabulary when expressing thoughts and emotions. For instance, words like “view”, “DJ”, and “chill” are commonly used to describe various aspects of their experiences.
