Bài tập về nhà

Homework · Monday, Wednesday, Friday · about 45–60 minutes each, on a computer or a phone.

How it works: each assignment has the same tabs — 1️⃣ Từ vựng (vocabulary) · 2️⃣ Ngữ pháp (grammar) · 3️⃣ Chính tả (spelling) · 4️⃣ Nói (speaking) · 5️⃣ Đọc (a short story). Work through them in order, then open 📊 Nộp bài, type your name, tap ⬇ Download report and upload that file to Canvas with the completion code — the file includes your recordings and writing. Progress and recordings stay in your browser — use the same device to finish an assignment.

Goes with Elementary Vietnamese (Ngô Như Bình) — keep your book open for the lesson’s dialogues and grammar notes.
