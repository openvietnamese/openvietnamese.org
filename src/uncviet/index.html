About the Program

Why Vietnamese @ UNC?

Vietnamese is the language of over 100 million people worldwide — and of more than 20,000 of your neighbors right here in North Carolina. Whether you are drawn by family heritage, career goals, a love of Vietnamese culture, or simply the challenge of a new language, UNC Chapel Hill offers a supportive, research-informed path to real proficiency.

Vietnamese is written in Quốc Ngữ — a Romanized alphabet where diacritical marks indicate both tone and vowel quality. While tones take practice, Vietnamese grammar is elegantly streamlined: words never change form for tense, person, or case. Its vocabulary reflects a layered history — Austroasiatic roots, centuries of Chinese literary influence, and a modern lexicon shaped by French colonialism and global contact. Learners gain access to one of Asia's richest literary traditions alongside one of its most dynamic contemporary cultures.

"The UNC Vietnamese Language Program fosters real-world proficiency and humanistic growth, teaching not just what students can do in Vietnamese, but who they can become through it. We serve as a vital hub of connection and belonging for all whose lives touch Vietnam."

VIET 101 Elementary Vietnamese I 3 credit hours

An introduction to modern standard Vietnamese. Students develop foundational skills in speaking, listening, reading, and writing, with an emphasis on everyday vocabulary and basic grammatical structures. Task-based activities ground learning in real communicative situations from the first week.

Prerequisite: None — open to all Offered: Fall Instructor: Dr. Huy Phung Gen-Ed: Language Making Connections

Getting Started

Placement & Credit

Who needs placement? If you have any prior knowledge of Vietnamese — home use, high school study, self-study, or exchange — you must take the departmental placement test before enrolling. Students with no background whatsoever may enroll directly in VIET 101 each fall.

How Placement Works

  1. Sign up using the DAMES departmental webform. You will receive test logistics and dates by email.
  2. Take the test. Format and timing are provided in your confirmation. The test assesses reading, writing, and listening comprehension.
  3. Receive your result by email and register for the course that matches your placement level on ConnectCarolina.

Related Policies & Resources

  • DAMES Language Placement Hub — all languages, FAQs, and registration links
  • Registration & Waitlist FAQ — what to do if your section is full
  • Questions about your specific level? Email asia@unc.edu or visit Dr. Phung during office hours (New West 113).

Ready to find your level? The placement test takes approximately 30–45 minutes and is completed online.

Heritage & Background Speakers

Heritage Learners

Grew up speaking Vietnamese at home? You belong here. Heritage and background speakers are a valued part of UNC's Vietnamese program. You may arrive with strong listening and speaking skills alongside a desire to strengthen reading, writing, spelling, tones, and formal registers — and our curriculum is designed with exactly that progression in mind.

🏡 What we focus on together

  • Literacy & formal registers — spelling, diacritics, tone accuracy, and academic vocabulary, while fully honoring your home variety and dialect.
  • Bridging to content — as your proficiency grows, courses incorporate news media, film, literature, and community-based projects that connect to your background.
  • Task-based learning — real-world assignments like community interviews, media analysis, and cultural projects make your existing cultural knowledge an asset in class.

How to Start

Take the placement test — we will recommend the right entry point based on your actual proficiency, not assumptions about what you "should" know. Heritage speakers commonly place into VIET 102, 203, or higher.

Faculty

People

👤
Dr. Huy Phung (thầy Huy)
Teaching Assistant Professor of Vietnamese · Department of Asian and Middle Eastern Studies

Dr. Huy Phung directs the Vietnamese Language Program at UNC Chapel Hill, where he teaches the full course sequence from elementary through advanced levels. His teaching is grounded in task-based, student-centered pedagogy that builds real-world communicative competence — from navigating everyday services to analyzing Vietnamese media and literature.

His scholarly work focuses on task-based language teaching and heritage language education, with particular attention to how task design affects the performance and experience of Vietnamese heritage learners. He uses open educational resources across all courses, including openly licensed textbooks and authentic digital media.

Recent: Presenter, 5th International Conference on Heritage/Community Languages (Los Angeles, Feb 2026) — "Heritage Language Students' Performance and Perceptions of Task-Based Materials in the Classroom." Funded by the Carolina Asia Center.
Selected: Participant & Mentor, SEALC Speaking and Writing Assessment Development Workshop + Teachers as Researchers Publication Workshop, Harvard University, April 2026. One of a small cohort of Southeast Asian language faculty selected nationwide by the Southeast Asian Language Council.
Office Hours (Drop-in Café ☕): Meet with your instructors, ask questions, and get individualized support on coursework or language learning — see the VLC page for current times.

Campus Life

Clubs, Events & Community

The Vietnamese program at UNC extends well beyond the classroom. A vibrant network of student organizations, cultural events, and language practice opportunities lets you use Vietnamese every week — and connect with the broader community at Carolina and in North Carolina.

Student Organizations

🏮

Vietnamese Student Association (VSA)

UNC's Vietnamese Student Association hosts cultural programming, community service, and social events throughout the year. A natural home for both Vietnamese-American students and language learners.

🐉

Blue Crescent Lion Dance Group

Performs traditional lion and dragon dances at Tết celebrations and campus events. Open to all skill levels — no prior dance experience required.

🗣️

Vietnamese Language Circle (VLC)

A weekly informal gathering to practice Vietnamese conversation outside of class. Welcoming to all levels, from beginners to heritage speakers. See the VLC page for details, or check @uncviet for meeting times.

Recurring Events

🎊

Tết Celebration & Gathering

Annual Lunar New Year celebration featuring bánh tét making, folk games (bầu cua), lì xì decorating, fortune telling with Kiều, and more. Hosted in collaboration with VSA. Read about Tết 2026 →

🎮

Vietnamese Folk Games Day

Students and community members come together to play traditional Vietnamese games like ô ăn quan (mancala), bầu cua, and more — a fun cultural immersion open to all.

☕

Vietnamese Language Table (VLC)

Casual weekly meetup for anyone who wants to practice Vietnamese in a relaxed, informal setting. All levels welcome. Held on campus and occasionally on Zoom. Follow @uncviet for schedules.

📚

Giỗ Tổ Hùng Vương — Hùng Kings' Festival

Cultural program commemorating Vietnam's founding mythology and national heritage, with readings, presentations, and community conversation in Vietnamese and English.
