Before you enroll

Find your Vietnamese course level

This self-access test takes about 15–20 minutes. It checks your background, listening, reading, and speaking, and gives you a preliminary course recommendation — from VIET 101 through the advanced sequence.

  • Background survey — your language history and connection to Vietnamese
  • Listening — short audio clips with comprehension questions
  • Reading — short passages with comprehension questions
  • Speaking — a few recorded prompts (microphone required; typing is a fallback)
This test alone does not finalize your placement. Every result requires a short follow-up conversation (Zoom or in person) with the instructor to confirm the right course — this matters especially for heritage learners, whose spoken and literacy skills often differ. You'll get instructions for scheduling that at the end.
