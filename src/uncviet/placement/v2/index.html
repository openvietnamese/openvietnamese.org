Before you begin

How the test works

Read this once — a few sections behave differently from a normal quiz.

  • 🎧Listening — each clip plays a maximum of two times. Play it before you look at the options if you can.
  • 📖Reading — the passage stays on screen; take as long as you need.
  • 🪜These two sections get harder in steps and stop automatically once the level is clearly beyond you. Finishing early is normal and is not a bad result.
  • 🤔Every question has an "I don't know" option. Use it — a guess that happens to be right places you in the wrong course.
  • ✍️Cloze — type one Vietnamese word per blank. Tone marks aren't required for credit; write them if you can.
  • 🎙️Sentence repetition — you hear each sentence once, then recording starts automatically after a 3-second countdown. Repeat as much as you caught, even if you don't understand all of it.
  • 🗣️Speaking & writing samples — not scored. Your instructor reads and listens to these before your conversation.
  • 📥At the end, download the ZIP and submit it to the Canvas assignment (or email it to the instructor), then book your placement conversation.
  • 🌐Use Chrome or Edge — Vietnamese speech recognition doesn't run in Safari. You can still record there; your instructor just scores it by hand.
  • 🚫No dictionary, translator, or outside help. This isn't a graded exam, but an inflated result costs you a semester in the wrong class — answer honestly.
